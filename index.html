Privacy policy

This policy explains what Apex Technologies Limited collects when you use our products and services, why we collect it, and the choices you have. This is demo content generated for testing purposes and is not a real legal document.

01Overview

Apex Technologies Limited ("Apex", "we", "us") builds software products and developer tools. This policy covers the personal data we process when you visit our website, create an account, or use our applications and APIs.

By using our services, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here. If you don't agree, please don't use our services.

02Data we collect

We collect information in three ways: what you give us directly, what we gather automatically, and what we receive from third parties.

Information you provide

  • Account details — name, email address, company, password
  • Billing information — billing address, payment method (processed by our payment provider; we don't store full card numbers)
  • Support requests — anything you include in a ticket, email, or chat with our team
  • Content you upload or create within our products

Information collected automatically

  • Device and browser type, IP address, operating system
  • Usage data — pages visited, features used, session duration
  • Log data from our servers and APIs, including error reports

Information from third parties

  • Single sign-on providers (Google, GitHub) if you choose to authenticate that way
  • Analytics and infrastructure partners listed in the section below

03How we use it

We use collected data to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our products
  • Process transactions and send related information, including confirmations and invoices
  • Respond to support requests and communicate about your account
  • Monitor and analyze usage trends to improve performance and user experience
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraudulent transactions and other illegal activity
  • Send product updates and marketing communications, where you've opted in

05Storage and security

Data is stored on infrastructure located in the European Union and United States. We use encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest, role-based access controls, and regular security reviews to protect your information.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. While we work to protect your data, we can't guarantee absolute security.

06Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Request a portable copy of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details at the end of this policy. We'll respond within 30 days.

07Cookies and tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember preferences, and understand how our services are used. You can control cookies through your browser settings, though disabling them may affect functionality.

08Data retention

We keep personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide our services. After account closure, we retain data only as required to meet legal, accounting, or reporting obligations, then delete or anonymize it.

09Children's privacy

Our services aren't directed at anyone under 16. We don't knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we'll delete it.

10Changes to this policy
